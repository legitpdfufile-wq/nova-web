NOVA / CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Your visit.
A separate space.

Public conversation

Your messages are sent to Joshua’s locally running Nova visitor service. The service uses an isolated visitor session, not the owner’s private memory. Conversation state is held in service memory and expires after inactivity; browser session storage retains recent messages on that browser tab. Use New chat to clear your tab’s conversation and start a new session.

What visitors can access

Conversation, approved Collection navigation, demo walkthroughs and links to existing checkout pages. Visitors cannot access owner files, private memories, system commands, administrative controls or account credentials through this workspace.

Voice and files

Microphone and speech features depend on your browser and its speech services; they are not represented as fully offline. Demo files are handled by each demo as described on its page. Avoid entering passwords, private records or other sensitive information in public chat.

Availability and ownership

The live conversation depends on the owner’s computer, local model service and public connection. Demos remain available separately. Copyright © 2026 Joshua Hale / MYNX Intelligence. No patent, trademark registration or exclusive rights over general AI concepts are claimed by these notices.
