03 / THE BUILDER

It started with
“Can we make a game?”

I started on a 4GB Chromebook, trying to make a game with my daughter. Each obstacle led to another tool: game builders, agents, creative workflows, then the infrastructure to connect them.

That way of working stuck. I learn by building, testing, finding the weak point and working through it. Today, that work has grown into MYNX Intelligence and a broader production and operating ecosystem.

The point is to make something people can actually use.
