Help, downloads & privacy

Clear answers before you download or buy.

Choose the edition that fits your device

Browser experiences open here; some are full free apps and some are labeled previews. Windows editions are separate downloads. Check the individual product page for current pricing, supported hardware and external requirements. AI Reader is free in both editions. Studio Film Desk is included in Studio Brain, not a separate purchase.

After a purchase

  1. Complete checkout on the linked Payhip listing.
  2. Use the download page or the link in your receipt email. Check spam/junk if the email is missing.
  3. Download the maintained customer package, not a file labeled legacy/reference.
  4. Extract the complete ZIP into a writable folder. Open its START HERE instructions and named launcher.
  5. Complete any stated setup, then try the main task. Local AI editions need their listed models/tools; buying does not install those automatically.

A Windows ZIP will not run as a phone app. Unsigned independent applications may show a Windows notice; review the source and requirements before deciding whether to run one.

Get human help

For a missing download, startup problem, compatibility question or refund request, contact the store or email MYNX Intelligence. Include the product, device/Windows version, order reference if relevant and the error text. Do not send passwords, card details, private files or identification. Purchase terms and any applicable refund conditions are shown by the store; this page does not promise an additional policy.

Community apps

MingleKind currently uses founder invitations and ChatGPT sign-in. OpenHeart is a separate adults-only connection app with its own free account. Both are 18+; review their own access, privacy and community guidance. OpenHeart and Nova conversation depend on the builder’s local operation; the browser tools and download pages do not require that laptop to be on.

Anonymous usage counts

When the visitor service is available, we count page views and navigation, store and download clicks by product and a broad source such as Facebook or TikTok. These are events, not unique people or completed purchases. The counter stores totals only: no text, filenames, email addresses or visitor IDs. It uses no analytics cookie; temporary campaign labels stay in this browser tab. Counts may be incomplete when the local service is offline or requests are blocked. Payhip handles its own checkout records.

Your files and conversation

File-reading demos process selected files in your browser. Some workspaces keep records in this browser and offer export or backup. Nova chat sends your typed or transcribed message and current-demo context to our guide service; it does not grant visitors access to private Nova operator tools. Browser voice features may use your browser’s speech services. Payhip and linked public apps have their own access and privacy information.

If Nova is taking a moment

The greeting and direct demo guidance are quick. Open-ended replies use local AI and can take about a minute. A visible progress display shows the reply state. You can continue browsing; after a message is acknowledged, its pending reply follows you between Collection pages. Walkthroughs remain available when chat is unavailable.

Keep your phone icon

Open the Collection homepage and choose Save / share. Add the Collection to your home screen using Safari’s Share menu on iPhone or Chrome’s browser menu on Android. The Collection icon and your existing NOVA Public Apps icon can both stay on your phone.
