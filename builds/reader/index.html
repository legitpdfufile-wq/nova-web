Privacy & getting sound working

This page does not upload your text or save it after you leave. Speech is handled by your browser and selected voice. Some voices use an online service; device voices are labelled when the browser identifies them. For installed Windows voices, use the Windows edition.

No sound? Check volume and audio output, choose another voice, then press Read aloud again. Open this page in a recent Edge, Chrome or Safari browser if an in-app social browser blocks speech. On phones, keep the page open while listening. Scanned images and protected documents need selectable text first.

THE ORIGINAL WINDOWS WORKFLOW

Select. Press. Listen.

Keep AI Reader in your tray. Select text in another app and press Ctrl + Alt + R. It copies that selection and reads it through an installed Windows voice.

You also get a text box, voice and speed controls, pause and stop. No paid AI account or model download. Windows 10/11 with a working installed speech voice; built-in .NET Framework 4.8. No separate runtime download on the tested Windows setup.

Download Windows edition · Free ↓Setup & help ↗

Extract the ZIP → open “AI Reader” → run AIReaderApp.exe. First launch may show Windows’ unsigned-app warning. Close hides it to the tray; use the tray menu → Exit to quit. The selection shortcut replaces clipboard text just like Copy. Some apps block copying; paste text into the reader instead.

One clear job.

AI Reader turns text into speech. It doesn’t summarize, answer questions or extract text from images. The browser edition is fully usable; the Windows edition adds the global selected-text shortcut.
